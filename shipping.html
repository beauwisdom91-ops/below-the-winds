Media Mail from Johnson.

Orders placed on this site are packed the same day or next morning. Default service is USPS Media Mail, from Johnson, Arkansas. A typical 1–2 lb book leaves in a kraft or rigid mailer with a charcoal label.

Handle time is one business day. We email to confirm the copies and payment before the parcel leaves.

Condition is named in the first sentence of every listing: highlighting, underlining, stamps, cocked spines. Defect notes when they matter. No filters. Each title is one used copy unless the catalog says otherwise.

If you bought here and the note was wrong, write shop@belowthewinds.com within 14 days of delivery. We will refund or replace from the shop — this is not an Amazon A-to-z claim. Unopened returns for change of mind are not the default; ask before you send a book back.

Media Mail is a United States service for books. It is slower than Priority. For other destinations, write before you order.
