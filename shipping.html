Fulfillment

Media Mail from Johnson.

Shop-till orders (card checkout on this site) ship US only. Shipping is a flat $4.99 USPS Media Mail, collected at checkout with the street address. Pack same day or next morning.

Amazon orders use Amazon’s own shipping and buyer messages. Default service is still USPS Media Mail. A typical 1–2 lb book leaves in a kraft or rigid mailer with a charcoal label.

Handle time is one business day. That metric keeps the seller account alive.

Condition is named in the first sentence of every listing: highlighting, underlining, stamps, cocked spines. Defect photos when they matter. No filters.

If a note was wrong, say so — the copy will be refunded fast. Amazon purchases follow Amazon’s used-book return rules. Direct purchases: email the shop within 14 days.
