Media Mail from Johnson.

Orders placed on Amazon are packed the same day or next morning. Default service is USPS Media Mail. A typical 1–2 lb book leaves in a kraft or rigid mailer with a charcoal label.

Handle time is one business day. That metric keeps the seller account alive.

Condition is named in the first sentence of every listing: highlighting, underlining, stamps, cocked spines. Defect photos when they matter. No filters.

Returns follow Amazon’s used-book rules. If a note was wrong, say so — the copy will be refunded fast.
