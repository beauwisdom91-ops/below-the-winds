How it works

Read the note. Add the copy. We pack it here.

You choose a used copy from the catalog, add it to the cart, and send a hold. We confirm by email, then pack from Johnson. Default service is USPS Media Mail. Handle time is one business day.

Card and PayPal are config slots. When a Stripe Payment Link or PayPal.me is pasted into js/config.js, those buttons light up on checkout. Until then, the hold form is the till.

Amazon is still the scale register, Individual, Fulfilled by Merchant, store name BelowtheWinds. Sell from sell.amazon.com. Do not turn on FBA.
