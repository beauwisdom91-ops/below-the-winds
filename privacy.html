Privacy

What the shop keeps.

Contact, want-list, and copy-request forms go to Netlify Forms and then to the shop email. That is name, email, and the message you typed.

Card payments are processed by Stripe. This site never sees card numbers. Stripe receives the book title, amount, email, phone, and US shipping address so the copy can be mailed.

Amazon purchases are Amazon’s privacy policy, not this one.
